For Federal and State Managers

Public-service leadership has its own employment rules and deadlines.

A grievance, proposed action, EEO matter or administrative appeal can affect a leadership role, professional standing and career. Avodah helps senior federal and state managers identify the forum, the next deadline and the decision that needs attention.

Start With the Notice and the Date

Do not assume that an internal discussion pauses a formal deadline.

The available process may depend on the employer, appointment, personnel action, forum and document received. Preserve the notice and identify every stated response, counseling, grievance or appeal date. An attorney must confirm the applicable procedure for the specific matter.

Government Leadership Matters

The right path begins with the agency, the leadership role and the forum.

01

Federal EEO matters

Discrimination, harassment and retaliation concerns that may proceed through an agency's federal-sector EEO process and later review.

02

MSPB and proposed actions

Proposed removals, suspensions and other personnel actions affecting managers or supervisors that may involve an agency response, Merit Systems Protection Board review or related strategy.

03

State manager grievances

Leadership disputes, discipline and grievances that may be governed by Virginia agency procedures, appointment status and administrative rules.

04

Administrative proceedings

Hearings, appeals and agency processes involving a leadership role, professional standing or another government decision affecting continued work.

05

Discrimination and retaliation disputes

Claims involving protected status, protected activity, whistleblowing or alleged retaliation, including matters that may proceed to litigation.

06

Professional-license intersections

Employment concerns that overlap with licensure, credentialing, reporting duties or proceedings before a Virginia licensing authority.

A Useful First Conversation

Orient the matter before sending the file.

Begin with the people or organizations involved, the general issue and the deadline. Do not send confidential records until Avodah confirms that it can speak with you.

  • The agency or public employer
  • Your leadership role, supervisory responsibilities and appointment status
  • The general type of action or complaint
  • The date and title of the notice received
  • Every stated response, counseling, grievance or appeal date
  • Whether a union, EEO counselor or agency representative is already involved
