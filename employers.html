Decision Map

Before discipline, separation or a major policy change, ask:

Avodah helps leadership turn these questions into a sequence, not a scramble.

  1. What decision are we actually making?
  2. Who has comparable facts or treatment?
  3. Is there protected activity, leave, accommodation or another legal overlay?
  4. What documentation exists, and what is missing?
  5. Does an investigation need independence?
  6. Who should communicate the decision?
  7. What happens in the first week after action?
