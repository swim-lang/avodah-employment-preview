Physician Employment Matters

The work may involve a document, a dispute, a license or all three.

Agreements and compensation

Employment and recruitment agreements, compensation structures, call obligations, repayment terms, ownership interests, restrictive covenants and negotiation priorities.

Licensing and credentialing

Professional-license concerns, credentialing questions, reporting obligations and administrative processes that may affect continued practice.

Investigations and disputes

Workplace investigations, allegations of misconduct, discrimination or retaliation claims, contract disputes and litigation strategy when a matter has escalated.

Transitions and restrictions

Termination, resignation, practice changes, patient continuity, notice duties, repayment exposure and restrictions that may affect the next opportunity.

When an Agreement Is Part of the Matter

Read the document as part of the working relationship.

01

Compensation and measurement

Base pay, productivity, RVUs, collections, quality metrics, bonuses, repayment obligations and the information available to verify performance.

02

Clinical and operational expectations

Schedule, call coverage, location, support, administrative duties, outside activities and changes the employer may make.

03

Professional protection

Licensure and credentialing obligations, malpractice coverage, tail coverage, records, reporting, peer review and regulatory considerations.

04

Change and departure

Term, renewal, termination, notice, compensation after notice, patient transition, property return and post-employment obligations.

05

Restrictions and future work

Confidentiality, nonsolicitation, noncompete language, moonlighting, ownership interests and the practical effect on the next opportunity.

Healthcare restrictions deserve current analysis.

After Conflicts Are Cleared

What to bring to the review.

  • Offer letter and full agreement
  • Compensation exhibits or plan documents
  • Restrictive-covenant documents
  • Equity, ownership or recruitment agreements
  • Policies incorporated into the agreement
  • Current agreement, if this is a transition
  • The decision deadline and the two or three outcomes that matter most
The Result of a Useful Review

A prioritized decision, not a pile of comments.

Avodah organizes issues by consequence and negotiability. The goal is to show what is standard, what is unusually important for this role, what may deserve revision and what tradeoff follows each choice.
